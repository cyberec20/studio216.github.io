PLANS & PAYMENT

Pricing FAQ

Which plans does DentiTrace offer?

Monthly and Annual are recurring subscriptions. Lifetime, when available, is a one-time purchase without renewal. Available plans, prices and included allowances are shown in the cards on this page and confirmed before payment.

Is Lifetime always available?

Not necessarily. Lifetime is a one-time purchase that may be available as part of the current offer. Check this page for availability. Its duration and minimum guarantee are governed by the Terms; it does not promise perpetual service.

Do monthly and annual subscriptions renew automatically?

Yes. Your monthly or annual subscription renews according to the billing cycle you selected until you cancel it. You can manage it from the subscription-management option in the pricing area or account menu. If a permanent shutdown is announced, future renewals will be stopped and obligations for existing paid periods will be addressed.

How do I cancel or resume a subscription?

Open the subscription-management option from the pricing area or account menu. You can cancel a monthly or annual subscription, and you can resume it when that option is available. After cancellation, automatic renewal is stopped, but your paid access continues through the end of the current billing period. After that, some plan-dependent actions may be limited while your existing clinic information remains available for viewing. Product-wide shutdown differs from voluntary cancellation by a Clinic and is governed by the Terms.

Is the Lifetime plan a one-time payment?

Yes. Lifetime is a one-time purchase for the Clinic without recurring renewals. It provides access for the commercial lifetime of DentiTrace, with a guaranteed minimum of three (3) years from activation. It does not mean perpetual service or ownership of the software. See the Terms for shutdown conditions.

Can plan details change over time?

The pricing page shows the plans, prices, storage, and availability currently offered. If a price or plan condition changes, you will be informed before it affects a future renewal through the email associated with your account and, when appropriate, a clear notice in DentiTrace. Your current paid period continues under the plan you selected, and a change is not applied retroactively. Before the next renewal, you can review the updated information and choose the option that best suits your needs. Lifetime is available only while it is offered. A Lifetime purchase retains its agreed terms and minimum guarantee, without renewals or retroactive changes arising from new offers.

Can I change from one subscription plan to another?

Yes. If you are subscribed monthly or annually, use the subscription-management option to review or change your plan. Your existing subscription is managed there, so you do not need to start a second one.

What happens to my plan if DentiTrace permanently closes?

Notice will be sent by email and other suitable channels. New purchases and renewals will stop; existing paid subscriptions will ordinarily run through their current term or applicable remedies will apply. Commercial Lifetime has a guaranteed minimum of three years from activation. Available instructions and options for retaining clinical data will also be communicated. See the Terms.

How do I request a refund?

The voluntary 14-day commercial guarantee starts with the clinic's first eligible subscription purchase; Monthly ↔ Annual switches and renewals do not reset it. After the last paid period actually expires, a later purchase may regain eligibility following 90 consecutive days without paid access. The first Lifetime purchase, when offered, carries its own guarantee. After a confirmed Lifetime refund, another Lifetime guarantee requires a 90-day cooling-off period from that refund, even if Monthly/Annual is purchased or renewed. See the Refund Policy at /dentitraceRefund Policy.html for conditions, exceptions and statutory rights.

How are payments processed?

Payments are completed through the secure checkout shown when you choose a plan. Once your payment is confirmed, the plan you selected is applied to your account. You do not need to take any additional payment step inside DentiTrace.

Where do I manage my subscription?

Use the subscription-management option in the pricing area or account menu. It opens the secure subscription-management page for your active plan. If there is no active subscription to manage, the application will indicate that no subscription is currently available for management.
