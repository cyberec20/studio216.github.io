PLANS & PAYMENT

Pricing FAQ

Complete answers about trial access, subscriptions, Lifetime availability, payment processing and refunds.

Subscriptions and Lifetime

Which plans does DentiTrace offer?

Monthly and Annual are recurring subscriptions. Lifetime, when available, is a one-time purchase without renewal. Available plans, prices and included allowances are shown in the cards on this page and confirmed before payment.

Is Lifetime always available?

Not necessarily. Lifetime may be offered at our discretion and may not always be available. When offered, the applicable conditions are established in our Terms and Conditions.

Is the trial free, and what happens when it ends?

Your trial lasts 21 days. When it ends, access to some clinical features is limited while the information already in the clinic remains available for viewing according to your permissions. You can choose a paid plan to restore full access.

What can I still do after the trial ends?

After the trial ends, you can continue to review existing information. Some actions for creating new users, patients, doctors, appointments, or encounters may be unavailable until you choose a paid plan. Demo-data actions may also depend on the access available to your clinic at that time.

How much storage does the trial include?

The trial includes 0.05 GB of storage. Paid-plan allowances appear on the cards on this page. Existing records are not automatically deleted when a storage allowance is reached.

Do monthly and annual subscriptions renew automatically?

Yes. Your monthly or annual subscription renews according to the billing cycle you selected until you cancel it. You can manage it from the subscription-management option in the pricing area or account menu. If a permanent shutdown is announced, future renewals will be stopped and obligations for existing paid periods will be addressed.

How do I cancel or resume a subscription?

Open the subscription-management option from the pricing area or account menu. You can cancel a monthly or annual subscription, and you can resume it when that option is available. After cancellation, automatic renewal is stopped, but your paid access continues through the end of the current billing period. After that, some plan-dependent actions may be limited while your existing clinic information remains available for viewing. Product-wide shutdown differs from voluntary cancellation by a Clinic and is governed by the Terms.

Is the Lifetime plan a one-time payment?

Yes. Lifetime, when available, is a one-time purchase without recurring renewals. It grants access under the conditions established in our Terms and Conditions and does not transfer ownership of the software.

Can plan details change over time?

The pricing page presents the plans, prices, storage allowances, and availability currently offered. If a price or plan condition changes, affected customers will be informed before the change applies to a future renewal. Existing paid periods remain subject to their agreed conditions. Lifetime purchases, when offered, remain subject to the conditions accepted at purchase and are not changed retroactively by subsequent offers.

Can I change from one subscription plan to another?

Yes. If you are subscribed monthly or annually, use the subscription-management option to review or change your plan. Your existing subscription is managed there, so you do not need to start a second one.

What happens to my plan if DentiTrace permanently closes?

We will provide notice through email or other appropriate channels. New purchases and renewals will stop. Existing paid subscriptions and any Lifetime purchases will be handled according to our Terms and Conditions and applicable law. Information about available data export options will also be provided.

Payment problems and refunds

What happens while a payment or plan change is pending?

While a payment or plan change is being confirmed, your account may temporarily show that the request is processing. Give the confirmation a moment to finish before trying another payment action. If the status does not update, use the support contact shown for your account.

What happens if a recurring payment fails?

If a recurring payment cannot be completed, you may be asked to update your payment method. Check your subscription status and use the subscription-management option or contact support if the issue continues.

How do I request a refund?

The voluntary 14-day commercial guarantee starts with the clinic's first eligible subscription purchase; Monthly ↔ Annual switches and renewals do not reset it. After the last paid period actually expires, a later purchase may regain eligibility following 90 consecutive days without paid access. The first Lifetime purchase, when offered, carries its own guarantee. After a confirmed Lifetime refund, another Lifetime guarantee requires a 90-day cooling-off period from that refund, even if Monthly/Annual is purchased or renewed. See the Refund Policy at /dentitrace/refund-policy.html for conditions, exceptions and statutory rights.

Checkout and payments

How are payments processed?

Payments are completed through the secure checkout shown when you choose a plan. Once your payment is confirmed, the plan you selected is applied to your account. You do not need to take any additional payment step inside DentiTrace.

Where do I manage my subscription?

Use the subscription-management option in the pricing area or account menu. It opens the secure subscription-management page for your active plan. If there is no active subscription to manage, the application will indicate that no subscription is currently available for management.
