PLANS & PAYMENT

Pricing FAQ

Reference content: November 13, 2026 (application pricing FAQ v1.0). Review subscriptions, Lifetime eligibility, payment handling and refunds before choosing a plan.

Subscriptions and Lifetime

Which plans does DentiTrace offer?

Monthly and Annual are recurring subscriptions. Lifetime, when available, is a one-time purchase without renewal. Available plans, prices and included allowances are shown in the cards on this page and confirmed before payment.

Is Lifetime always available?

Lifetime is not always available. When offered, you can review the applicable Terms and Conditions before purchasing.

Is the trial free, and what happens when it ends?

Yes. The initial trial is free and lasts 21 days. If your clinic earns valid benefits through the referral program while its trial is still active, the trial end date may be extended within the program's limits. Days that cannot be applied directly may remain in the referral balance for later redemption, where eligible. Once the effective trial period ends, and if the clinic has no other active access, certain clinical features become restricted. Existing records remain available for viewing according to the applicable permissions. You can subscribe to a paid plan to restore the features it includes.

Will I be charged automatically when my free trial ends?

No. The free trial does not automatically turn into a paid subscription, and you are not required to purchase a plan. When your effective trial access ends, some creation or editing features may become restricted, while viewing existing records remains subject to your permissions. To access paid features, you must explicitly select and purchase a plan.

What can I still do after the trial ends?

After the trial ends, you can continue to review existing information. Some actions for creating new users, patients, doctors, appointments, or encounters may be unavailable until you choose a paid plan. Demo-data actions may also depend on the access available to your clinic at that time.

How much storage does the trial include?

The trial includes 0.05 GB of storage. Paid-plan allowances appear on the cards on this page. Existing records are not automatically deleted when a storage allowance is reached.

Do monthly and annual subscriptions renew automatically?

Yes. Your monthly or annual subscription renews according to the billing cycle you selected until you cancel it. You can manage it from the subscription-management option in the pricing area or account menu.

How do I cancel or resume a subscription?

Open the subscription-management option from the pricing area or account menu. You can cancel a monthly or annual subscription, and you can resume it when that option is available. After cancellation, automatic renewal is stopped, but your paid access continues through the end of the current billing period. After that, some plan-dependent actions may be limited while your existing clinic information remains available for viewing. Cancelling a subscription does not automatically request a refund; these are separate actions.

Is the Lifetime plan a one-time payment?

Yes. Lifetime is a one-time purchase for your clinic, without recurring renewals. You can access DentiTrace during its commercial lifetime, subject to the Terms and Conditions applicable to your purchase.

Can plan details change over time?

Current plan options, prices, features, and terms are shown before purchase. If the terms for new purchases change, you can review the applicable terms before buying. Changes that may affect an existing subscription or an upcoming renewal will be handled according to the agreed terms, the Terms and Conditions, and applicable legal notification requirements. The terms of an already paid period will not be changed retroactively. Lifetime purchases, when available, retain the terms applicable at the time of purchase.

Can I change from one subscription plan to another?

Yes. If your clinic has a current, verifiable Monthly or Annual subscription, you can review the available changes through subscription management, provided the provider's portal is available for that account. Changes are managed on the existing subscription, without starting a separate second subscription. If the portal is unavailable or the subscription's status needs verification, contact support before attempting another purchase.

Payment problems and refunds

What happens while a payment or plan change is pending?

While a payment or plan change is being confirmed, your account may temporarily show that the request is processing. Give the confirmation a moment to finish before trying another payment action. If the status does not update, use the support contact shown for your account.

What happens if a recurring payment fails?

If a recurring payment cannot be completed, you may be asked to update your payment method. Check your subscription status and use the subscription-management option or contact support if the issue continues.

How do I request a refund?

When a purchase meets the eligibility requirements, DentiTrace displays “Request refund” among your account options. Select it and confirm the request. The system checks the transaction again and submits it to the payment provider; the provider's confirmation may still be pending. See the Refund Policy for the applicable terms.

Checkout and payments

How are payments processed?

Payments are completed through the secure checkout shown when you choose a plan. Once your payment is confirmed, the plan you selected is applied to your account. You do not need to take any additional payment step inside DentiTrace.

Where do I manage my subscription?

Use the subscription-management option in the pricing area or account menu. It opens the secure subscription-management page for your active plan. If there is no active subscription to manage, the application will indicate that no subscription is currently available for management.
