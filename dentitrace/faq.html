Product and access

Who is DentiTrace for?

DentiTrace is intended for dentists, dental clinics, and authorized clinical or administrative team members who manage clinic operations and patient care records. The clinic remains responsible for determining which authorized members may access and manage those records.

Can I use DentiTrace with more than one clinic?

Yes. A user can belong to more than one clinic and work within the clinic currently selected for the session. Records and permissions remain separated by clinic.

How do I switch between clinics?

Click the clinic chip in the top-right area of the header, which displays the name of your active clinic. The “Switch clinic” dialog will show the active clinics available to you; select another clinic to change your workspace. The selected clinic becomes the context for the records, team, and permissions shown to you.

What happens when a clinic invites me?

You can accept a matching pending invitation from the clinic invitation flow. Acceptance can create a new membership or reactivate a previously removed membership, using the role and permissions defined by the invitation. The invitation lifetime is 72 hours.

What are demo data for?

Demo data gives the clinic a safe way to explore DentiTrace, understand its workflows, and become familiar with patient records, clinical histories, appointments, and other features before working with real information. Demo records are clearly identified as demo data so they can be distinguished from the clinic's real records.

Can I explore the application with demo data before creating real records?

Yes. An owner can load a prepared demo environment from the onboarding or dashboard experience, review representative clinical workflows, and later clear the demo records when the clinic is ready to work with its own information.

What happens when demo data are cleared?

The product previews the demo records that will be cleared and requires confirmation. Records created by the clinic as real data, along with its settings, remain in place.

Who can load or clear demo data?

Demo-data management is available to clinic owners and administrators. Clearing the data uses a confirmation step so the clinic can review what will be removed first.

Does DentiTrace issue electronic invoices or integrate with national tax systems?

DentiTrace is first and foremost a clinical application. While there are specialized programs for invoicing and tax compliance, DentiTrace focuses on what matters most in your practice: creating treatment budgets, tracking partial payments, and monitoring the financial status of every case—all within your clinical workspace. These internal financial tools give you complete control over your clinic's operations. For official tax reporting, you'll use your preferred billing solution alongside DentiTrace.

Who can manage clinic members?

Only members with the required team-management authority can invite, update, or remove other members. The exact actions depend on the member's role and permissions.

What happens if my membership in a clinic changes?

Your access is managed through your current clinic membership. If that membership ends, your access through that clinic ends and active clinical-session invitations associated with it are closed. Access can be restored if the clinic invites you again.

What happens to the data and storage of my new clinic?

The new clinic starts as an independent workspace. Its patients, documents, settings, members, and storage limits are managed separately from the other workspaces you belong to. Data is not copied automatically between clinics.

What happens to clinic data if DentiTrace permanently shuts down?

Instructions and available options to preserve, consult or export clinical data will be communicated in accordance with Service capabilities and applicable law. For payment and subscription consequences, see the Plans FAQ and Terms.

Referral program

Is the referral program available?

When the referral program is available, you receive a referral code and link that you can share with a new clinic.

How can I earn additional trial days?

Share your clinic’s referral link with a new clinic. When that clinic registers through the link and completes the required confirmation, both clinics receive 7 additional trial days, subject to the program limit.

How many additional days can I earn through referrals?

You can accumulate up to 35 additional days through successful referrals. Added to the initial 21-day trial, this represents the maximum total extension available through the program.

Does the clinic I invite also receive a benefit?

Yes. When a new clinic registers through your link and completes the required confirmation, it receives 7 additional trial days, and your clinic receives the same amount, subject to the program limit.

What happens to referral days I have not used yet?

Earned referral rewards remain in the referral balance when they cannot be applied immediately. The procedure and limits for subsequent redemption are not yet defined; the balance does not automatically restart the trial or change subscriptions.

Privacy, terms, and help

Why might I be asked to confirm the Privacy Policy or Terms again?

The product may ask you to confirm the current Privacy Policy or Terms when one of those documents has been updated or when your account has not yet recorded confirmation for the current version.

What happens if I have not confirmed the current Privacy Policy or Terms?

You may need to confirm the current document before continuing with certain protected features. Once you confirm it, you can continue using the features available to your account.

What can I check if something does not work as expected?

First check that you are in the correct clinic, have the required permission, have confirmed any requested consent or current Privacy Policy or Terms, and have enough available storage. If the issue continues, contact support at dentitrace@studios216.com or mailto:dentitrace@studios216.com.

Permissions and clinical records

Why might an action be unavailable to me?

Actions are available according to your permissions within the currently selected clinic. An action may need a particular role, a compatible record status, or completion of a required consent or account step.

Who can create or change clinical information?

Authorized clinic members can perform the clinical actions allowed by their role and permissions. The selected clinic and the record's current status also determine which actions are available.

What information can DentiTrace help a clinic manage?

The current clinical workspace includes patient records, encounters, odontograms, diagnoses, treatment plans, prescriptions, consents, appointments, and generated clinical documents. The clinic and its professionals remain responsible for the accuracy and appropriateness of clinical decisions.

Which diagnostic coding systems are available?

The clinical workspace provides baseline ICD-10 and ICD-11 diagnostic catalogs. The application does not expose national modifications such as U.S., Canadian, Australian, or other country-specific versions. Clinics can search and use the catalogs available in the application; adding a different national modification is not currently supported.

How many diagnoses are included in the ICD-10 and ICD-11 catalogs?

DentiTrace includes 170 diagnoses from ICD-10 and 295 diagnoses from ICD-11. The difference reflects natural evolution: ICD-11 (WHO, 2022) is more granular than ICD-10 (1990), with more specific categories for dental conditions—giving you sharper clinical precision when you need it. Both catalogs are ready to use. Pick the level of detail that fits your practice.

Which tooth notation and treatment catalogs are supported?

The odontogram supports FDI and Universal tooth notation, with conversion between the two systems. The clinical workspace also provides a baseline treatment catalog. Authorized users may search available treatments and manage tenant-specific treatment information according to the permissions and customization options available in the application.

Why do I need to confirm consent before viewing the patient list?

Confirming the active consent step allows the patient directory to load its rows and related statistics.

Is my consent acceptance recorded?

Yes. Your acceptance is recorded for the selected clinic and consent version, together with the date and time of acceptance. The product also keeps audit information associated with that acceptance, such as your IP address and browser details.

How does consent work together with patient access?

Consent is one part of the access process. Your clinic membership, permissions, and the requested action also determine what you can view or change.

What happens when an encounter is completed or cancelled?

When an encounter is completed or cancelled, the product captures a historical snapshot. The snapshot preserves the relevant clinic, provider, branding, timezone, and signature information for that record.

Can a historical clinical document change when clinic information changes later?

Once an encounter is completed or cancelled, it becomes read-only. Its historical record preserves the patient, provider, and clinic information captured at that time. Later changes to clinic or professional profiles do not alter the historical record or the documents generated from it. To continue working with the patient, create a new encounter.

Can I invite someone to an active consultation?

An authorized clinic member can invite another person to an in-progress clinical session. The invitation provides time-limited collaboration within that consultation.

How many people can be invited to one active consultation?

Up to three active invitations can exist for one session, with one active invitation per email address.

How long does a consultation invitation remain valid?

The invitation link is valid for one hour. When sending the invitation, the doctor can indicate an estimated collaboration duration between one and twenty-four hours. Access ends when the clinical session closes, or earlier if the invitation is revoked.

Clinic records and documents

Can I download clinical PDFs?

Yes. Depending on your permissions and the type of clinical information, you can download readable PDF documents such as prescriptions, treatment plans, specialty reports, consent documents, and consolidated records from historical encounters. Historical views can also provide PDF downloads for past clinical information.

Can I download a patient's historical record as a PDF?

Authorized users can download consolidated PDFs for historical encounters and other supported clinical documents when the corresponding access permissions are available. These downloads make a patient's clinical history readable and portable.

Appointments and scheduling

Can I create appointments and manage availability?

You can manage appointments and professional availability from the calendar area. The actions available to you depend on your clinic permissions and the selected clinic.

Why might I be unable to assign or modify an appointment?

Appointment changes depend on your current clinic membership, the relevant scheduling permission, and the appointment's current status. If a change is unavailable, check that you are in the correct clinic and have the required permission.

What happens when I cancel an appointment?

When you cancel an appointment, its time slot becomes available again for scheduling. The appointment is removed from the active calendar, so you can use that time for another appointment.

Supported assets and storage

What kinds of files or images can I add?

DentiTrace supports specific application assets such as profile photos, patient/avatar photos where enabled, clinic logos, professional signatures, and stamps. General uploads of patient PDFs, scans, or image collections are not currently supported.

What are clinic logos, signatures, and stamps used for?

They provide clinic or professional identity assets for supported application views and generated clinical documents. Their use depends on the relevant clinic and professional permissions.

What happens when a clinic reaches its storage limit?

When the storage allowance is reached, actions needing extra space may be temporarily limited. Existing clinical data are not automatically deleted. Contact dentitrace@studios216.com or mailto:dentitrace@studios216.com for help. Plan-specific allowances appear on the Plans page.

What happens if an image or asset fails validation?

If an asset does not meet the supported type, size, or security checks, the product asks you to adjust it before trying again.
