Product and access

Who is DentiTrace for?

DentiTrace is intended for dentists, dental clinics, and authorized clinical or administrative team members who manage clinic operations and patient care records. The clinic remains responsible for determining which authorized members may access and manage those records.

Can I use DentiTrace with more than one clinic?

Yes. One person can belong to several clinics and switch between them using the active-clinic selector. Each clinic has separate patient records, documents, team members, permissions, storage, and paid-access terms. Switching clinics does not transfer records or subscription plans between them.

Does my DentiTrace subscription cover every clinic I belong to?

No. Each clinic has its own records, permissions, storage allowance, and paid-access terms. When you switch clinics, you see the plan and features available to that clinic; one clinic's subscription does not automatically transfer to another. Purchases are managed by someone with billing authority for the selected clinic.

How do I switch between clinics?

Click the clinic chip in the top-right area of the header, which displays the name of your active clinic. The “Switch clinic” dialog will show the active clinics available to you; select another clinic to change your workspace. The selected clinic becomes the context for the records, team, and permissions shown to you.

What happens when a clinic invites me?

You can accept a matching pending invitation from the clinic invitation flow. Acceptance can create a new membership or reactivate a previously removed membership, using the role and permissions defined by the invitation. The invitation lifetime is 72 hours.

What are demo data for?

Demo data gives the clinic a safe way to explore DentiTrace, understand its workflows, and become familiar with patient records, clinical histories, appointments, and other features before working with real information. Demo records are clearly identified as demo data so they can be distinguished from the clinic's real records.

Can I explore the application with demo data before creating real records?

Yes. An owner can load a prepared demo environment from the onboarding or dashboard experience, review representative clinical workflows, and later clear the demo records when the clinic is ready to work with its own information.

What happens when demo data are cleared?

The product previews the demo records that will be cleared and requires confirmation. Records created by the clinic as real data, along with its settings, remain in place.

Who can load or clear demo data?

The clinic owner can create and remove demo data from the Dashboard Summary. The service also authorizes administrators to perform these operations, although their Dashboard does not currently display that control. Before removing demo data, DentiTrace previews the affected records and asks for confirmation.

Does DentiTrace issue electronic invoices or integrate with national tax systems?

No. DentiTrace does not issue tax invoices or integrate with national tax systems. You can prepare treatment estimates, record partial payments, review outstanding balances, and generate internal receipts. To issue tax invoices, use your usual invoicing solution.

Who can manage clinic members?

Only members with the required team-management authority can invite, update, or remove other members. The exact actions depend on the member's role and permissions.

What happens if my membership in a clinic changes?

Your access is managed through your current clinic membership. If that membership ends, your access through that clinic ends and active clinical-session invitations associated with it are closed. Access can be restored if the clinic invites you again.

What happens to the data and storage of my new clinic?

Each clinic has an independent workspace for its patients, clinical records, generated documents, settings, and team members. It also has its own storage allowance for the features and resources supported by DentiTrace. Information and storage are managed separately, and data is not shared or automatically copied between clinics.

Referral program

Can I recommend DentiTrace and redeem my days?

When the referral program is enabled for your clinic, you can share the code and link shown in the application. If you have days in your bank and are eligible to redeem them, you can use the Referrals button. Eligibility depends on your subscription and server verification.

How are referral days awarded?

Share your clinic's link with a new clinic. If it registers through the link and completes the required confirmation, it receives a one-time welcome reward of 7 days; your clinic earns up to 7 own-reward days for that referral while earning capacity remains. Merely sharing the link does not award days, and adding staff to an existing clinic is not a new referred clinic.

What is my earning limit if I keep referring clinics?

Your clinic can earn up to 35 of its own referral-reward days over its participation, under the configured limit. Historically awarded days count even after they have been used or redeemed; emptying the bank does not reset the limit. Once the limit is reached, further eligible invitees can still receive welcome days, but your clinic earns no more. If less than 7 days of capacity remain, only the remaining amount is awarded.

Does an invited clinic receive welcome days after I reach my limit?

Yes. Each eligible new clinic receives its one-time welcome reward of 7 days, regardless of whether you have reached your own earning limit. This welcome reward does not use your limit, nor does it reduce that clinic's ability to earn its own referral rewards later.

What happens to referral days that cannot extend my trial?

Valid rewards that cannot extend an unexpired trial remain in the clinic's referral bank. Receiving rewards does not restart an expired trial. Banked days are not a paid subscription, do not alter Lemon, and are not erased simply because you stop signing in. If you meet the redemption requirements, you can use the Referrals button to redeem your entire available balance.

How do I redeem the days in my bank?

If the server confirms you are eligible, you can voluntarily redeem your entire available balance in one operation, with no day selector or partial redemption. Before confirming, you can see the days to be used and the remaining balance. The start and end dates of bonus access are confirmed when redemption is processed. Having days in the bank does not guarantee redemption if paid access or another restriction still applies.

Can I redeem days while on Monthly, Annual, or Lifetime?

Not while verified paid or residual access is in effect, including Lifetime, or while a payment or refund issue is unresolved. Legitimate rewards can remain in the bank. Once paid access has actually ended, the system checks your clinic’s commercial status, balance, and permissions again to allow redemption when eligible.

Do redeemed days pause or return to the bank if I buy a plan?

No. Once redemption is confirmed, its bonus-access period follows verifiable UTC start and end times. It provides trial-like access, not a Monthly, Annual, or Lifetime subscription. Buying another plan does not pause that clock or return used days to the bank, and does not automatically change the confirmed redemption dates.

Do welcome days and earned rewards guarantee one continuous trial?

Not necessarily. The initial trial is 21 days under the applicable configuration. Direct extensions of an unexpired trial have limits and may include both welcome days and rewards you earned; days that cannot be applied directly remain in the bank. The 35-day limit applies to historical own rewards, not a guarantee of additional uninterrupted trial time for everyone.

Will I lose my days if I stop logging in or the program ends?

Inactivity alone does not remove recorded referral-bank days. This does not guarantee that the service or program will operate forever: a permanent program closure would require separate terms, notice, and treatment, subject to the Terms and applicable law. Referral days are not cash and do not, by themselves, create a purchase-refund right.

Privacy, terms, and help

Why might I be asked to confirm the Privacy Policy or Terms again?

The product may ask you to confirm the current Privacy Policy or Terms when one of those documents has been updated or when your account has not yet recorded confirmation for the current version.

What happens if I have not confirmed the current Privacy Policy or Terms?

You may need to confirm the current document before continuing with certain protected features. Once you confirm it, you can continue using the features available to your account.

What can I check if something does not work as expected?

First check that you are in the correct clinic, have the required permission, have confirmed any requested consent or current Privacy Policy or Terms, and have enough available storage. If the issue continues, contact support at dentitrace@studios216.com or mailto:dentitrace@studios216.com.

Permissions and clinical records

Why might an action be unavailable to me?

Actions are available according to your permissions within the currently selected clinic. An action may need a particular role, a compatible record status, or completion of a required consent or account step.

Who can create or change clinical information?

Authorized clinic members can perform the clinical actions allowed by their role and permissions. The selected clinic and the record's current status also determine which actions are available.

What information can DentiTrace help a clinic manage?

The current clinical workspace includes patient records, encounters, odontograms, diagnoses, treatment plans, prescriptions, consents, appointments, and generated clinical documents. The clinic and its professionals remain responsible for the accuracy and appropriateness of clinical decisions.

Which diagnostic coding systems are available?

The clinical workspace provides baseline ICD-10 and ICD-11 diagnostic catalogs. The application does not expose national modifications such as U.S., Canadian, Australian, or other country-specific versions. Clinics can search and use the catalogs available in the application; adding a different national modification is not supported.

How many diagnoses are included in the ICD-10 and ICD-11 catalogs?

DentiTrace includes 170 ICD-10 diagnoses and 295 ICD-11 diagnoses organized to support coding in dental practice. You can search for and record the available diagnoses according to your clinic's features and permissions.

Which tooth notation and treatment catalogs are supported?

The odontogram supports FDI and Universal tooth notation, with conversion between the two systems. The clinical workspace also provides a baseline treatment catalog. Authorized users may search available treatments and manage tenant-specific treatment information according to the permissions and customization options available in the application.

Why do I need to confirm consent before viewing the patient list?

Confirming the active consent step allows the patient directory to load its rows and related statistics.

Is my consent acceptance recorded?

Yes. Your acceptance is recorded for the selected clinic and consent version, together with the date and time of acceptance. The product also keeps audit information associated with that acceptance, such as your IP address and browser details.

How does consent work together with patient access?

Consent is one part of the access process. Your clinic membership, permissions, and the requested action also determine what you can view or change.

What happens when an encounter is completed or cancelled?

When an encounter is completed or cancelled, the product captures a historical snapshot. The snapshot preserves the relevant clinic, provider, branding, timezone, and signature information for that record.

Can a historical clinical document change when clinic information changes later?

Once an encounter is completed or cancelled, its historical record becomes read-only and preserves the patient, clinic, professional, and document context captured for that encounter. Later profile changes do not rewrite that snapshot. This does not prevent authorized updates to the patient's current information or the creation of new encounters; those actions do not retroactively change the closed encounter.

Can I invite someone to an active consultation?

An authorized clinic member can invite another person to an in-progress clinical session. The invitation provides time-limited collaboration within that consultation.

How many people can be invited to one active consultation?

Up to three active invitations can exist for one session, with one active invitation per email address.

How long does a consultation invitation remain valid?

The invitation link must be accepted within 15 minutes of its creation. When sending it, the clinician can set a collaboration duration of between 30 minutes and 24 hours. Once accepted, access ends when the authorized period expires, calculated from the invitation's creation, or earlier if the clinical session is closed or access is revoked.

Clinic records and documents

Can I download clinical PDFs?

Yes. Depending on your permissions and the type of clinical information, you can download readable PDF documents such as prescriptions, treatment plans, specialty reports, consent documents, and consolidated records from historical encounters. Historical views can also provide PDF downloads for past clinical information.

Can I download a patient's historical record as a PDF?

Authorized users can download consolidated PDFs for historical encounters and other supported clinical documents when the corresponding access permissions are available. These downloads make a patient's clinical history readable and portable.

How can I share a consolidated encounter PDF with a patient?

If you have the required permissions, you can download the consolidated PDF from the encounter history and send it manually using an authorized clinic communication channel. Before sharing it, verify the recipient's identity and address and follow applicable confidentiality and consent requirements.

What does an encounter's consolidated PDF contain?

It brings together the available clinical documents for the selected encounter, based on the information recorded and your access permissions. This may include consultation details, specialties, treatments, prescriptions, consent forms, and the odontogram where applicable. It does not add sections or documents that do not exist for that encounter. Each encounter retains its own historical context.

Appointments and scheduling

Can I create appointments and manage availability?

You can manage appointments and professional availability from the calendar area. The actions available to you depend on your clinic permissions and the selected clinic.

Why might I be unable to assign or modify an appointment?

Appointment changes depend on your current clinic membership, the relevant scheduling permission, and the appointment's current status. If a change is unavailable, check that you are in the correct clinic and have the required permission.

What happens when I cancel an appointment?

When an appointment is cancelled, it no longer occupies its time slot on the active calendar. That slot may be booked again if the professional's availability, other commitments, and scheduling permissions allow it. Cancellation remains subject to applicable controls and audit records.

Supported assets and storage

What kinds of files or images can I add?

DentiTrace supports specific in-app assets, such as profile or patient photos where enabled, clinic logos, professional signatures, and stamps. Available uploads depend on the relevant forms and validations. It does not provide a general-purpose folder for patient PDFs, X-rays, diagnostic studies, or arbitrary file collections.

What are clinic logos, signatures, and stamps used for?

Clinic logos, signature images, and professional stamps help identify the clinic and its professionals in compatible clinical documents. Once configured, DentiTrace can include them in those documents according to the context and applicable permissions.

What happens when a clinic reaches its storage limit?

If a clinic reaches its storage allowance, actions that require additional space may be limited until capacity becomes available again. Existing clinical records are not automatically deleted because the quota is reached. The allowance depends on the clinic's plan and any storage add-ons it actually has active. For assistance, use DentiTrace's contact channels.

What happens if an image or asset fails validation?

If an asset does not meet the supported type, size, or security checks, the product asks you to adjust it before trying again.
