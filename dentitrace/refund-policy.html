Refund Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

DentiTrace is a SaaS platform for dental clinics and their teams. Payments are processed through Lemon Squeezy, subject to its terms and applicable law. This policy defines the voluntary 14-day full-refund commercial guarantee for Monthly and Annual plans and for Lifetime, when offered or previously purchased. The guarantee depends on the clinic's commercial history and the specific purchase: it does not renew with every charge.

Refund Eligibility

You may request a full commercial refund within 14 days IF one of these conditions applies:

  • This is the clinic's first confirmed commercial purchase of a Monthly or Annual plan. The 14 days start with that purchase, not the start of the trial.
  • The clinic purchases Monthly or Annual again after its previous paid access has ended and 90 consecutive days have passed without an active paid-access entitlement.
  • This is the clinic's first Lifetime purchase: it has its own 14-day guarantee, even when replacing an active Monthly or Annual subscription.
  • The transaction is confirmed, belongs to the requesting clinic and the request is made within the eligible period, without limiting applicable statutory rights.

Special case: Lifetime

These conditions apply when Lifetime is available for purchase or when it was previously purchased.

  • Lifetime is a one-time purchase without recurring renewal. Its first eligible purchase carries a separate 14-day commercial guarantee, including for existing subscribers.
  • After a confirmed Lifetime refund, another Lifetime purchase does not automatically create a new commercial guarantee for the following 90 days. That period begins when the refund is confirmed and is NOT paused or reset by purchasing or renewing Monthly/Annual.
  • A Lifetime repurchase after those 90 days may qualify again for the 14-day commercial guarantee, subject to history verification and objective fraud or abuse checks. Unlimited automatic refunds are not promised.
  • A Lifetime refund ends access arising from that purchase. A previous Monthly or Annual paid period may continue only until expiry if still paid, unrefunded and verified; its renewals are not automatically reactivated.

Outside the commercial guarantee

NO new automatic commercial guarantee is granted IF:

  • The 14-day period for the eligible purchase has ended. Renewals and Monthly ↔ Annual changes do not reset it.
  • Monthly or Annual is purchased again before 90 days without paid-access entitlement have passed since the previous relationship actually expired, unless an earlier guarantee is still valid.
  • Lifetime is repurchased less than 90 days after a confirmed Lifetime refund, without limiting mandatory statutory rights.
  • The transaction is unconfirmed, has already been refunded or does not belong to the stated clinic and purchase.

How the 90 days are counted

  • Returning to Monthly/Annual: the 90 consecutive days without paid access begin when the last paid period actually expires, not when cancellation is requested.
  • Repurchasing Lifetime after a Lifetime refund: the 90 days start when the refund is confirmed. Purchasing or renewing Monthly/Annual during that interval does NOT interrupt it; a later confirmed Lifetime refund starts a new cooling-off period.
  • The clinic may still sign in and may purchase again before the 90 days are completed; doing so does not by itself grant another commercial guarantee. Neither the one-time trial nor referral-awarded days constitute purchases or reset guarantees.

Clarifications

Eligibility is assessed by clinic and transaction, considering earlier purchases, plan changes and refunds. Canceling a subscription normally prevents future renewals but does not by itself remove an already paid period; canceling is not requesting a refund. Monthly ↔ Annual switches preserve the commercial relationship. Mandatory statutory rights and decisions within the merchant of record's authority remain separate from this voluntary guarantee. Valid rights arising from earlier purchases are not retroactively reduced.

How to Request a Refund

If a purchase is eligible, use the “Request refund” option in your account or its purchase link. If unavailable or you need to raise an applicable statutory right, contact contact@studios216.com with your clinic and transaction reference. The request will be assessed under the applicable conditions and payment provider procedures.

Consumer Consent

By purchasing a plan and starting the service, you expressly consent to begin using the digital service immediately. Any statutory right of withdrawal applies according to applicable law and the circumstances of your purchase.

Refund Processing

Receiving a request does not mean approval. A refund is confirmed when Lemon Squeezy processes it; returning funds to the original payment method is subject to its timelines. A confirmed refund may terminate entitlements from the refunded purchase and cancel future renewals for the affected subscription.
