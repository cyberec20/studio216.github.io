Privacy and analytics

Last updated: September 25, 2026.

Studios216 uses non-essential analytics only after you choose Accept analytics. If you reject non-essential analytics, the website remains available and Google Analytics 4, Meta Pixel and Microsoft Clarity are not loaded by the Studios216 analytics layer.

What we use

Google Analytics 4 (GA4) helps us understand website traffic and aggregate usage. Microsoft Clarity helps us understand interaction patterns and usability. Meta Pixel is reserved for commercial measurement such as product CTA or external product visits; it is not used for every custom analytics event.

Product-interest events

The site is prepared to measure a small event taxonomy when product components are introduced: product_impression, product_click, product_cta_click and external_product_visit. These events are used to understand which content leads readers to explore Studios216 products.

Your choice

Your analytics choice is stored in your browser under a Studios216 consent key. You can reopen the consent panel at any time using the Privacy choices button shown on the site. Rejecting after previously accepting reloads the page so non-essential providers are not loaded again.

Important: provider-specific cookies or identifiers, retention periods and data processing are governed by the provider and by the settings of the corresponding Studios216 account. This page does not claim a retention period that cannot be verified from the public website repository.
