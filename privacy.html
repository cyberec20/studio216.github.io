Privacy and analytics

Last updated: September 26, 2026.

Studios216 uses a mixed consent model. Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity load in limited, cookieless/no-consent modes by default so we can retain basic aggregate measurement. Meta Pixel remains blocked until you choose Accept analytics.

What we use

Google Analytics 4 (GA4) uses Google's advanced Consent Mode. With analytics storage denied, the Google tag can send measurements without analytics cookies for basic measurement and modeling. Microsoft Clarity uses ConsentV2 and can operate in no-consent mode without Clarity cookies, with limited tracking. Meta Pixel is not loaded until analytics consent is accepted; after acceptance it records page views and its custom events remain limited to commercial actions such as product CTA or external product visits.

Product-interest events

The site is prepared to measure a small event taxonomy when product components are introduced: product_impression, product_click, product_cta_click and external_product_visit. These events are used to understand which content leads readers to explore Studios216 products.

Your choice

Your analytics choice is stored in your browser under a Studios216 consent key. You can reopen the consent panel at any time using the Privacy choices button shown on the site. Rejecting after previously accepting returns GA4 and Clarity to denied-storage mode and reloads the page so Meta Pixel is no longer loaded.

Important: provider-specific cookies or identifiers, retention periods and data processing are governed by the provider and by the settings of the corresponding Studios216 account. This page does not claim a retention period that cannot be verified from the public website repository.
