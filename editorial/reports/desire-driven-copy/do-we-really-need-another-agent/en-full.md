## v0.9.1 interpretation

- Discover motive and current conversation before writing.
- Harvest missing proof before increasing rhetoric.
- Clarify vague objections before reframing.
- Prefer proof over rhetoric when proof resolves the concern.
- A BLOCK from Reframe Integrity or Autonomy requires revision before use.
- Diagnose weak certainty surfaces before adding urgency.
- If Prospect Fit is weak, narrow or stop rather than increase pressure.
- Treat action friction as a product/process problem when appropriate.
- Adapt sequence to involvement, knowledge, skepticism, and decision stage.
- A BLOCK from Covert Manipulation requires revision before use.
- Use reader-generated inference only when the evidence clearly supports it.
- Pair material negative consequences with credible efficacy and a real path forward.
- Design what the reader should understand and remember, not only what the seller wants to say.
- For delayed or trial-based decisions, define a memory footprint and a trial/evaluation path.
- Internalization requires reader-owned reasons; seller pressure is not a substitute.
- If adoption matters, optimize beyond the purchase event.

> Automated scores are diagnostics, not proof of persuasive effectiveness. Actual evidence, offer terms, reader autonomy, context, and real conversion data outrank the heuristic.
